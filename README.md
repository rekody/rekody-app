# Rekody

Voice dictation for macOS that **never leaves your Mac**. Hold a key, speak,
release — your words land at the cursor, in any app.

### [⬇ Download for Mac](../../releases) · macOS 13+ · Apple Silicon & Intel

## What it is

- **Local & private by default** — your audio is transcribed on your Mac. Nothing is uploaded.
- **Instant** — words stream in as you talk (on-device streaming engine).
- **Works everywhere** — anything that accepts text.
- A floating HUD pill shows your live dictation — drag it anywhere, hold to talk, or quick-tap to go hands-free.

## Free app, open-source core

The Rekody **app** is **free to use** ([EULA](EULA.md)); its source is private.
The **engine and command-line tool are open source (MIT)** —
[github.com/rekody/rekody](https://github.com/rekody/rekody) — so you can read
exactly how transcription works, or run it yourself from the terminal:
`brew install rekody/rekody/rekody`.

## Install

1. Download the latest `.dmg` from [Releases](../../releases).
2. Open it and drag **Rekody** into Applications.
3. Launch it and follow the one-time setup (grant microphone + accessibility, pick an engine).

## Public beta

Rekody is in public beta. Bug or feature request? [Open an issue](../../issues).

## Privacy

Dictation runs on-device by default — your voice and transcripts stay on your
Mac. A separate, optional paid service can fine-tune a model on data you choose
to upload, under its own terms, with delete-by-default handling.
